Everything Claude knows is text in one window

Claude doesn't see your screen. It sees one long stack of text called the context window: your messages, its own notes, and the result of every tool it runs.

When Claude reads a Notion page or a web page, that text lands in the same stack as your words. Nothing marks it as "a stranger wrote this."

Key idea Anyone who can put text in front of Claude can try to give it orders. That's what prompt injection is.
